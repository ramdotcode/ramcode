Questions before you pack

Still unsure about something? Ask us on WhatsApp, a person answers within the hour.

Do I pay anything before I see my plan?

No. Your first itinerary and quote are free. You pay a 20% deposit only after you approve the plan, and the balance 30 days before departure.

Are international flights included?

Package prices cover stays, transfers, guided tours and entry tickets. We can quote and book flights too, listed as a separate line so you can compare.

Can I change the itinerary after booking?

Yes. Swap hotels, add a day or drop an activity for free up to 21 days before you travel. After that we still help, and only pass on what suppliers charge us.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Cancel 30 days or more before departure and your deposit comes back in full. Closer to the date, refunds follow each hotel and tour's own policy, which we show you before you pay.

Who helps me if something goes wrong on the trip?

Your own travel designer, plus a 24/7 WhatsApp line staffed by real people. Missed connection, lost bag, sudden storm: we rebook and reroute while you grab a coffee.

Tell us where your heart is set. We'll handle the rest.

Four quick answers and your request lands in our WhatsApp. A travel designer replies within an hour, every day from 8am to 9pm.
